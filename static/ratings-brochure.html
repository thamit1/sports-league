The Process

How Ratings Are
Calculated

Every match result feeds the engine. Every result is fair, transparent, and mathematically consistent.

1

Match Is Played & Scored

Officials record the final score in SLMS. The system captures the exact score margin — not just win or loss — giving a richer picture of performance. Each sport uses its own scoring logic (points, runs, frames, legs).

2

Engine Runs Recalculation

A super admin triggers recalculation. The engine replays every match in chronological order — computing expected outcomes, actual results, and updating each player's rating using the Elo formula adapted for a 0–100 scale.

3

Rankings Update Instantly

After recalculation, all leaderboards refresh — global, per-club, by age group, by division. Players see their new rank, rating change, and full history. Clubs see their squad's performance at a glance.

The Math

Proven Elo
Mathematics

SLMS uses a battle-tested Elo algorithm adapted to a 0–100 scale. Every update is fair, deterministic, and auditable. No black boxes — every delta is stored in history.

Expected Score Formula
E =  1 / (1 + 10 ^ ((Rb - Ra) / 40))
# Ra = your current rating (0–100)
# Rb = opponent's current rating (0–100)
# E = probability you win (0.0 to 1.0)
New Rating Formula
delta =  K × (Actual − Expected)
delta =  clamp(delta, −MaxChange, +MaxChange)
NewRating =  clamp(OldRating + delta, 0, 100)
Score Margin — Sport Specific
Points sports →  score_a / (score_a + score_b)
Goal sports →  0.5 ± min(diff, cap) / cap × 0.5
Chess →  1.0 win / 0.5 draw / 0.0 loss
Tug of War →  binary 1.0 / 0.0 only

For Players

Your Skill,
Measured.

No more guessing where you stand. Your SLMS rating is a real, honest reflection of your performance — sport by sport.

  • 📊

    Separate Rating Per Sport

    Your Badminton rating never affects your Chess rating. Each sport is tracked independently, so you can be a Beginner at Cricket and an Expert at Table Tennis simultaneously.

  • 🎯

    Singles & Doubles Tracked Separately

    Your individual skill in singles is measured independently from your team coordination in doubles. Both ratings count — both matter.

  • 📈

    Full Rating History & Trend

    See every match's impact on your rating. Watch your curve trend upward over months. Know exactly which opponents helped you grow and which exposed gaps.

  • 🏅

    Ranked in Multiple Scopes

    See your rank globally, within your club, in your age group, and in your division — all simultaneously. Compete at the level that matters most to you.

For Clubs

Run a Smarter Club

Ratings give club admins powerful new tools — from fair team selection to competitive scheduling to data-driven coaching decisions.

⚖️

Fair Team Selection

Use ratings to seed teams evenly across tournaments. No more debates about who should play — the numbers speak objectively.

📅

Balanced Match Scheduling

Schedule matches between players with similar ratings for close, competitive games that are fun for everyone and build skill.

🔍

Talent Identification

Spot fast-rising players whose rating is climbing quickly. Identify talent early before they're on anyone else's radar.

📣

Showcase Your Best

Publish leaderboards to celebrate top performers. Recognition motivates participation and builds club pride.

📊

Club-Level Analytics

See how your club's average rating compares across divisions, age groups, and sports. Make data-backed decisions on coaching focus.

🔄

Season Reset Flexibility

Each season you can carry ratings over, do a soft reset toward midpoint, or hard reset — configured per sport to match your competition format.

For Super Admins

Total Control.

Every rating parameter is configurable per sport. Recalculation runs on your schedule, not automatically — keeping you in full control of when and how ratings publish.

⚙️

Per-Sport Configuration

Set K-factors, provisional thresholds, visibility rules, starting ratings, and max change per match independently for each sport.

🔄

Manual Recalculation

Ratings only update when you trigger recalculation. Run it for one sport or all sports at once. Full audit log kept for every run.

👁️

Visibility Control

Decide who sees ratings for each sport: public to everyone, visible to club members only, or restricted to admins.

🗓️

Season Management

Configure hard reset, soft reset, or carry-over per sport at season end. Soft reset gently pulls everyone toward midpoint for a fresh but fair start.
